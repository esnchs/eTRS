About This Web Portal

Your Online Portal for Document Transmittals

This website is the central online platform of the eTransmittal Record System, built to fast-track document routing between the School, District, and Division Office. Through this web portal, users can track real-time processing statuses and view published digital record entries online—ensuring complete efficiency, transparency, and accountability across every office level.

🔍

Live Web Tracking

Search TRF numbers online to check whether your document is pending, released, or returned.

📖

Digital Record Book

Access a transparent online ledger of all official transmittals moving across offices.

📄

Digital Transmittal Slips

Generate and print official transmittal receipts straight from your browser.
